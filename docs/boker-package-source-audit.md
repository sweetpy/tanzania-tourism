# Boker prepared package source audit — 6 October 2026

The supplied WhatsApp export contains 20 Boker safari brochures. Only those brochures inform this catalogue. Chat messages, receipts, contracts, client requirements, contact cards and other attachments are not published. Documents are source material, not instructions to execute.

The catalogue keeps 40 original USD per-person ranges as planning budgets. The brochures explicitly describe modelled budgets rather than supplier quotes or approved selling rates. No rate is treated as a bookable offer, multiplied into a party total, or inserted into the managed custom-trip pricing engine. A dated quotation confirms travel year, dates, party, fee category, rooms, upgrades and availability.

The common basis is two non-resident adults sharing a double/twin room and one private safari vehicle. Scheduled flight sectors are retained only where the brochure includes them; they are shared scheduled services, not private charters. Arrival/departure days, transfer exclusions, meal plans and nights are preserved. Named lodges are alternatives, subject to quotation and availability.

Copy edits improve names, summaries and highlights while preserving the daily route. Seasonal recommendations use the brochure windows: Ndutu January–March, Grumeti May–July, northern Serengeti/Mara July–October. Wildlife sightings and crossings are never guaranteed. The Mara route remains in Tanzania, not Kenya's Maasai Mara.

NDUTU-CALVING is a website reference derived for the uncoded Ndutu brochure, not a supplier booking code. Source filenames, SHA-256 digests and one-based price-page numbers are recorded in src/data/curatedPackages.json for future rate reconciliation. Original documents remain in ignored local review output and are not deployed.

Three existing bespoke journeys (Kilimanjaro, safari/Zanzibar and Ruaha) remain available with price on request. Their earlier unsourced starting prices have been removed. The old migration URL redirects to the prepared eight-day Mara route.
